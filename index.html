Frequently Asked Questions

Aapke common sawaalon ke jawab

Booking kaise hoti hai? +

Bahut simple hai! WhatsApp pe contact karein ya website se form bhare. Package select karein, 40% advance payment ke baad aapki date confirm ho jati hai. Baaki 30% wedding se 1 week pehle aur 30% wedding day pe.

Photos aur videos kab milte hain? +

24-48 ghante me aapko 30-50 best sneak peek photos WhatsApp pe mil jaati hain. Full edited photos 20-45 days me aur cinematic wedding film 30-60 days me deliver hoti hai. Album 45-75 days me ready hota hai.

Kya outstation weddings cover karte ho? +

Haan bilkul! Sultanpur se 50 KM tak free hai. Uske baad travel + stay ka charge client ko arrange karna hota hai. Hum Varanasi, Lucknow, Prayagraj, Jaipur aur pan India cover karte hain.

Drone shoot included hai packages me? +

Royal (1 Lakh) aur usse upar ke packages me drone included hai. Classic package me drone add karna ho toh extra charge lagta hai. Drone permission aur weather conditions apply hoti hain.

Kya package customize ho sakta hai? +

Haan! Aap apni zaroorat ke hisaab se package customize kara sakte ho. Koi extra service add karna ho ya kuch hatana ho, sab flexible hai. WhatsApp pe call karein aur hum aapke budget ke hisaab se best deal banate hain.

Cancellation policy kya hai? +

30 din pehle cancel karne pe 70% refund milta hai. 15-30 din pehle cancel pe 50% refund. 15 din se kam me koi refund nahi hota. Date change free hai (subject to availability).
