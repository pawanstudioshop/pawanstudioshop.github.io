FAQs

Common questions about our work and process

How do I book Pawan Studio? +

Simply fill the inquiry form on this page or WhatsApp us directly. Select your package and pay 40% advance to confirm your date. The remaining 30% is due one week before the event and the final 30% on the event day.

When will I receive my photos and videos? +

You will receive 30–50 sneak peek photos within 24–48 hours. Full edited photos are delivered in 20–45 days, the cinematic film in 30–60 days and the album in 45–75 days.

Do you cover outstation weddings? +

Yes, we cover destinations across India. Travel within 50 km of Sultanpur is included. For outstation events, travel and accommodation are arranged by the client or charged separately.

Is drone coverage included? +

Drone coverage is included in the Royal package and above. It can be added to the Classic package at an extra charge. Subject to local permissions and weather conditions.

Can I customise a package? +

Absolutely. All packages can be tailored to your needs. Call or WhatsApp us and we will build a quote around your budget and event requirements.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Cancellations made 30+ days before the event receive a 70% refund. 15–30 days before: 50% refund. Less than 15 days: no refund. Date changes are free subject to availability.
